If the movement never registers, look at two things. First ms/frame: much above 400 and the camera is being sampled too slowly to catch a turn, which usually means the model fell back to the CPU. Second yaw — turn your head fully and watch how far it travels. Everything is measured against your resting pose, so where it starts does not matter; only how far it moves does. If a full turn moves it less than the target above, the target is wrong for this camera and should be lowered.
